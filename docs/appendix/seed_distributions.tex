\section{Per-seed distributions}
\label{app:seed_distributions}

\Cref{tab:fixed17_alignment} reports arithmetic means across retained systems after per-system seed-IQM summaries. The corresponding per-(system, seed) distributions are shown below. \Cref{fig:fixed17_seed_strips} shows the paper-facing strip plot for the matched boundary-emphasized rows. \Cref{fig:appfig_fixed17_perseed} plots per-(system, seed) histograms of forecasting MSE at every paper-facing horizon. \Cref{fig:appfig_fixed17_entropy} plots per-(system, seed) histograms of \(H(B\mid S_{\rm abs})\), \(H(S_{\rm abs}\mid B)\), and exact-support uniqueness on the deep-state slice. \Cref{fig:appfig_dysts_perseed} plots the same per-seed distributions for the Dysts \(dt{\times}30\) benchmark. The histograms show retained-system and seed-level dispersion around the paper-facing means.
