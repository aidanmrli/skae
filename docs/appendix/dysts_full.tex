\section{Dysts forecasting campaign status}
\label{app:dysts_full}

A cadence audit found that the historical KAE caches retained the native Dysts integration interval rather than the requested \(30dt\) interval, while the standalone controls used the intended cadence. Consequently, every historical Dysts result, ratio table, horizon curve, and significance annotation is quarantined and is not evidence in this manuscript. A corrected matched campaign retraining all six KAE rows at a numerically verified \(30dt\) cadence is in progress; no result from that campaign is reported here.
