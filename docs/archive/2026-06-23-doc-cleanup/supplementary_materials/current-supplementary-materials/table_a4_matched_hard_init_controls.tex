\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The matched hard-init controls rule out a LISTA-only interpretation of the
basin-support result. Under the same near-boundary sampling protocol, the sparse
MLP control has basin-pure absolute-threshold supports on the deep evaluation
slice and strong short-horizon forecasting, while the dense no-shrink MLP is
much worse functionally despite also producing non-mixing thresholded supports
on this selected slice. The causal guardrail is therefore that induced latent
sparsity, not the LISTA unrolling architecture alone, is sufficient to produce
support objects that align with evaluation basin labels and remain useful for
forecasting. Standard-sampling and near-boundary-sampling rows should remain
separate in the paper tables.
