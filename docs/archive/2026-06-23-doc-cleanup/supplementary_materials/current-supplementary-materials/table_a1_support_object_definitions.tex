\section{Support object definitions and sensitivity}
\label{sec:supp_table_a1_support_object_definitions}

All support objects in \Cref{tab:support_object_definitions_sensitivity}
are computed after training from the learned encoder output
\(z=\Enc(x)\). Basin labels, known basin counts, and trajectory-to-basin
assignments are evaluation-only metadata on benchmark systems. They are not
used to train the encoder or transition model, define \(S_{\rm abs}\),
\(S_{\rm top8}\), or \(S_{\rm rel}\), choose active coordinates, or construct
support families such as \(F_{\rm abs}\) and \(F_{\rm top8}\). When the table
mentions a dominant support or dominant group, that dominance is a post-hoc
display or scoring annotation against withheld benchmark labels.
